HOME ASSIST : A HOME SERVICES BOOKING PLATFORM

Finding a reliable person to fix a leaking tap, clean the house before a
guest arrives, service an air conditioner, or repair a switchboard is a
frustrating experience for most households.Usually we have to ask
friends for recommendations look at ads or search online but we never
really know if the person who shows up is good at their job, trustworthy
or even available. Also prices are unclear until the worker arrives,
there is no proof of the work done, and if something goes wrong there is
no one to complain to.

At the same time, skilled workers as electricians, plumbers, carpenters,
AC technicians, cleaners, pest control staff, and appliance repair
technicians, struggle to find a steady stream of customers. They depend
on local reputation and have no organized way to advertise their
availability, manage their daily schedule, or get paid securely without
disputes over cash.

Home Assist aims to solve this problem by acting as a trusted online
marketplace connecting customers who need home services with verified
service providers who can perform them.

A customer using Home Assist should be able to open the app, do a quick
login and browse the list of services available in their area (such as
plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, appliance repair, home decor, or
pest control), and book a convenient time slot. The customer should be
able to see the estimated price of the service before confirming the
booking, get a estimated time in which service provider will show up,
and pay securely through the app once the job is done either online or
in cash. After the service, the customer should be able to rate the
provider and leave feedback, and should be able to raise a complaint if
the work was unsatisfactory or if the provider did not show up.

A service provider using the platform should be able to register and get
verified by the platform (through document and background checks) before
they can accept jobs. Once verified, the provider should be notified of
nearby job requests, and should be able to accept or reject them based
on availability. The provider should be able to view their schedule for
the day, mark a job as \"in progress\" and \"completed,\" and receive
payment automatically once the customer confirms completion.

An admin/support team member should be able to manage users (both
customers and service provider), monitor all bookings, verify new
service provider applications, handle escalated complaints between
customers and providers, and manage the list of services and their
pricing.

The goal of Home Assist is to make booking a home service as simple,
fast, and trustworthy as booking a cab --- with transparent pricing,
verified professionals, and a clear system for accountability on both
sides.
